About

I've spent the last 8+ years working across software and data. Most days I'm connecting systems that were never designed to talk to each other, and making sure the data that comes out the other side is right.

I started out in data science and machine learning, and that still shapes how I build. These days I'm also perfecting my front-end skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, through the Udacity Front End Web Developer Nanodegree and the challenges below.
